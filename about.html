The Spark

We saw a continent full of dreamers being let down by broken promises.

Addro was born from frustration. The kind that keeps you up at night. We watched African entrepreneurs — brilliant, ambitious, hardworking — hand over their savings to tech agencies that treated them like transactions, not partners.

Projects started without understanding the business. Emails went unanswered for weeks. Codebases were built like sandcastles — beautiful until the first wave hit. And worst of all, the disappearing act: payment made, agency gone. No support. No maintenance. No accountability.

We knew Africa deserved better. So we built it.

The Observation

African businesses were being underserved by local tech agencies.

The Frustration

Poor code, zero communication, and agencies that vanish after payday.

The Decision

Build an agency that treats every client like a true partner.

The Result

Addro — 30+ projects, 20 businesses launched, 100% retention.
