Questions

Do I get the source files?

Always. You get AI, EPS, PNG, SVG, and PDF formats. We don't hold your brand hostage — once it's paid for, it's yours forever.

How many revision rounds?

2 rounds for Starter, 3 for Brand System and Total Package. Additional revisions are billed hourly. But our concept phase is tight — most clients nail it in round 1 or 2.

What if I only need a logo?

We can do a standalone logo project starting at $500. But honestly? A logo without a system is like a crown without a kingdom. We usually talk clients into at least the Starter package so their logo actually works everywhere.

How long does it take?

Starter: 1–2 weeks. Brand System: 3–4 weeks. Total Package: 5–6 weeks. Timelines depend on feedback speed — the faster you respond, the faster we ship.

Can you redesign my existing brand?

Absolutely. Rebrands are our favorite. We audit what's working, scrap what's not, and evolve your identity without losing the recognition you've already built.
