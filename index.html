The Problem &
Our Solution

1

Unclear Scope

Agencies frequently start a project without the full scope of the business, leading to mismatched expectations and costly revisions down the line.

The Addro Way

We don't just jump into a project. We hear about the client's perspective, put ourselves in their shoes, and make sure we fully understand every aspect before writing a single line of code.

2

Poor Response Time

Most African tech agencies take a long time to reply and have irregular update schedules, leaving clients in the dark about their own projects.

The Addro Way

Addro changes the game with steady replies and constant updates on every part of the project. You're never left wondering what's happening.

3

Low Code Quality

Most tech agencies deliver software that lacks modern-day scalability, security, or proper documentation — creating problems for future growth.

The Addro Way

We use top-notch software architecture that can take you loads of years without failures. Scalable, secure, and well-documented from day one.

4

The Disappearing Act

This is a common issue with most African tech agencies — payment made and just like that, they're gone. No support, no maintenance, no accountability.

The Addro Way

Here at Addro we stay with our clients, constantly checking up on them and making sure that their business is still up and running. We're partners, not vendors.
