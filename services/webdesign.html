Questions? Answered.

Do you also develop the website, or just design it?

This service is design-only. We deliver Figma files, prototypes, and developer-ready assets. If you already have a developer, they can build directly from our handoff. Need development too? Check our Web Development service — we offer a bundled package.

How long does a typical web design project take?

Most projects take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to final handoff. A simple landing page might be 10 days; a multi-page site with complex interactions can stretch to 6 weeks. We give you a clear timeline in our proposal before we start.

What if I don't have a brand identity yet?

No problem. We can design within a minimal direction (mood boards, reference sites, color preferences). However, for best results, we recommend our Branding + Web Design bundle. It ensures your site feels cohesive, not patched together.

Will my developer understand your Figma files?

Absolutely. We organize layers, name components properly, and include spacing annotations. We also offer a 30-minute handoff call with your developer to walk through the file and answer questions. Most devs tell us our files are the cleanest they've received.

Can I request more than 2 revision rounds?

Yes — additional revision rounds are billed at an hourly rate. But honestly, our discovery phase is thorough enough that 2 rounds usually gets us to "this is perfect." Major scope changes (new pages, new features) after approval are quoted separately.
