App Questions

Should I build native or cross-platform?

Cross-platform (Flutter/React Native) is faster and cheaper — great for MVPs and most business apps. Native (Swift/Kotlin) wins when you need heavy animations, AR, or platform-specific features. We'll recommend the right path based on your feature list and budget.

How long does it take to build an app?

MVP: 6–8 weeks. Full product: 12–16 weeks. Complex enterprise apps: 5–6 months. We break projects into sprints so you see working builds every two weeks — not just a big reveal at the end.

Do you handle app store submission?

Yes — it's included in every package. We prepare screenshots, descriptions, privacy policies, and handle the review process. If Apple or Google rejects the app, we fix the issues and resubmit at no extra cost.

Will I own the source code?

100%. Full GitHub repo access, documentation, and setup instructions. We don't use proprietary frameworks or lock you in. Any competent developer can pick up where we left off.

What about maintenance after launch?

Every app includes 60 days of post-launch support. After that, we offer maintenance retainers starting at $800/month — covering OS updates, bug fixes, feature additions, and store compliance updates. We don't ghost you after launch.
