FAQs

What software do you edit in?

Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve for video. Photoshop, Lightroom, and Capture One for photos. After Effects for motion graphics. We export in whatever format your platform needs — H.264, ProRes, RAW, you name it.

How do I send you my footage?

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or Frame.io — whatever works for you. For large projects, we'll set up a shared cloud workspace. We also accept hard drives if you're local to Lagos.

Can you match a style I like?

Absolutely. Send us reference videos, mood boards, or links to edits you love. We'll match the pacing, color grade, and energy — or create something original if you want to stand out from the references.

What if I need revisions after delivery?

Your package includes 2–3 revision rounds depending on tier. Additional revisions are billed at $50/hour. We recommend consolidating feedback into one list per round to keep things efficient.

Do you provide raw project files?

Yes, on request and for an additional fee (usually 20% of project cost). This includes Premiere timelines, After Effects projects, or layered PSD files. Most clients just need the final exports, but we don't gatekeep your work.
